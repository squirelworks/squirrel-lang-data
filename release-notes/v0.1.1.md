# squirrel-lang-data v0.1.1

The first update to the frozen v0.1.0 data. It only adds things. Nothing that
was already there changes shape, so an app on v0.1.0 can move to it without
other changes.

## What's new

- **Vocabulary linked to meanings** (`meaning/vocab-links/`). For the 15
  languages with a meaning column, vocabulary cards now list the meanings
  they carry, 773 links in all. They cover cards picked by hand plus every
  starter-deck card whose English is exactly a meaning's. LanguageStudentDesk
  uses the links to connect the word lists and the meaning matrix.
- **CEFR levels for European grammar.** French, German, Italian, Greek and
  Russian lessons each carry an estimated CEFR level, and each course has a
  plan of points from A1 to C2. Each language also gets a CEFR reference
  card that quotes the Council of Europe's level descriptions.
- **Where grammar markers go.** Each language's meta can now say whether its
  particles, prepositions, articles and measure words come before or after
  the word they mark. It's filled in for the 16 languages that have a
  meaning column, plus English. squirrelator uses it to group words into
  phrases.
- **SQLite language packs.** Each language is also exported as a single
  SQLite database, with its varieties in the same tables, so a question like
  "every JLPT N5 word" is a single query. These databases are attached
  alongside the plain-file packs.
