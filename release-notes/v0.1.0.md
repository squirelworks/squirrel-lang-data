# squirrel-lang-data v0.1.0

The first frozen release of the SquirrelGo language data. LanguageStudentDesk,
the SquirrelGo apps and squirrelator pin their `data/` submodule to this
release while the apps are tested for usability and accuracy. Fixes found in
testing will come out as v0.1.1, v0.1.2 and so on.

## What's in it

- **38 languages**, each with its meta, and regional and historical varieties
  where they exist (Aramaic with Classical Syriac and Neo-Aramaic, for
  example).
- **Vocabulary**: about 29,000 words. That includes the JLPT N5 to N1 lists
  for Japanese, the graded words of the NIKL Basic Korean Dictionary, and the
  most frequent Chinese words with CC-CEDICT pinyin and meanings, each tagged
  with its level and how common it is.
- **Grammar**: about 470 lessons. Japanese, Korean and Chinese lessons each
  carry an estimated JLPT, TOPIK or HSK level, along with a plan of the points
  still to write at every level. Aramaic has a first grammar.
- **Reference cards**: level cards for the JLPT, TOPIK and HSK that quote the
  official descriptions, and new Aramaic cards.
- **Character tables** for Chinese, Japanese and Korean characters.
- **The meaning matrix**: meanings, phrasings and example sentences shared
  across languages.
- **Feature switches** (`features.json`) for the Simple, More and Everything
  settings levels.
- **Language packs**: every language exported as plain files that other
  programs can open, attached to this release as one zip.

## Not in this release

Stroke-order data, character variants and programming courses still live in
LanguageStudentDesk. They'll move here in v0.2.0.
